Enroll your face first
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe src\main.py --enroll-face Owner


Look at the camera, move your head slightly, and let it collect samples.
Add another person
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe src\main.py --enroll-face Rahul


It will authenticate the owner first, then open the enrollment camera.
Manage registered faces
List users:
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe src\main.py --list-faces


Remove a user:
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe src\main.py --remove-face Rahul


The owner must authenticate before removal.
